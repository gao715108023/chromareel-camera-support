ChromaReel Camera Privacy

Updated November 11, 2026

ChromaReel Camera, also named 胶片暗房 in Simplified Chinese, captures, processes and stores photos on your device. The native camera and darkroom features work without an app account. Purchases and optional feedback use online services.

Photos, originals and editing settings

Originals, developed photos, thumbnails and editing recipes are kept in the app's local storage. Available aperture, ISO, shutter speed, focal length, exposure compensation, camera and lens models are read from photo EXIF on your device for display and export. Camera preferences, your language selection, export decoration choice and watermark settings are stored locally. A tapped focus point is used for the current camera session.

The camera and darkroom do not upload your rolls or recipes to the developer. Device backups may include app documents according to your iOS backup settings.

Pro purchases and export allowance

Pro is a one-time lifetime in-app purchase. Apple StoreKit verifies purchases and restored entitlements. Apple handles payments and Apple Account details. The developer does not receive your bank card details or Apple Account password.

The successful free export count is stored in the device Keychain to apply the 3-export allowance. It contains no photo content and is not used for advertising or tracking. This count is separate from your rolls. Deleting a roll or the app does not explicitly reset it. Device erasure and other system behavior may affect retention. The free allowance does not sync between devices. Pro can be restored using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Custom author text and selected camera parameters are stored locally. A signature or visible camera settings you put on an exported photo can be seen by its recipients. Watermark selections control visible text and leave other available photographic EXIF in the JPEG.

Optional feedback

Opening Feedback connects an embedded browser to Tencent Survey in Chinese or Google Forms in English. The selected provider processes the responses and files you choose to submit. Both forms ask for a feedback type and feedback details. The app does not require a name, email address or phone number in those forms. Google may offer optional sign-in to save your progress.

The provider also handles connection, browser and account information for its service under its own privacy policy. Tencent Survey describes processing such as IP addresses, answer timing, device and operating system details, referral information and applicable login state. Google explains its processing in the Google Privacy Policy. The form owner receives submitted responses through the selected service.

The app does not automatically attach local photos, rolls, camera settings or editing recipes to feedback. It uses a temporary browser session, without persistently saving the page's cookies or cache to app storage. Feedback pages load when you open Feedback.

Permissions

  • Camera access is requested for shooting.
  • Add-only Photos access is requested when you save an export.
  • Photo imports use the system picker and receive only the images you select.

You can import photos when camera access is denied. With a remaining free allowance or active Pro entitlement, you can share or save to Files when Photos access is denied. Manage permissions in iOS Settings.

Exports and deletion

Exports create developed JPEG photos and retain available photographic EXIF. GPS, device serial numbers and private maker notes are excluded from exported files. Recipients handle shared files under their own policies.

Deleting a photo from a roll removes its original, developed file, thumbnail and index entry in the app. Copies in Photos or Files are retained. Deleting the app removes its local storage. Export photos you want to keep first.

The built-in demo scene is AI-generated and labeled as a sample. The native app uses no advertising or tracking SDKs and requests no location permission.
