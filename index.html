ChromaReel Camera Support

Turn the dial. Develop your colors. A film-style camera with a mechanical film dial and a color darkroom for iPhone.

Switch between Chinese and English

Open the camera settings at the bottom right, then Guide & Privacy. Choose English or 简体中文 under App language. Your choice is remembered. The updated version includes both languages.

Restore Pro

Open Camera settings → Guide & Privacy → Upgrade to Pro, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase with no auto-renewal. It includes Little Light 400, unlimited exports and custom watermarks.

Free export allowance

New users receive 3 successful free exports. Saving to Photos, sharing and Save to Files share this allowance. Each successful export counts once, including repeat exports. Canceled or failed exports do not count.

Camera and Photos permissions

Check camera and add-only Photos permissions for the app in iPhone Settings. Imports use the system photo picker. The app asks for add-only Photos access when you save an export.

Where photos are stored

Originals, developed photos and editing recipes are stored in local film rolls. Export photos you want to keep before deleting the app. Copies already exported to Photos or Files are retained when you delete a photo from a roll.

Export borders and watermarks

Turn off both Paper border and White watermark to export a plain photo. The app remembers your selection. Pro users can set an author name and choose which available camera parameters appear. In English, signatures use “Photo by …”.

Landscape photos

The updated version saves photos upright using the physical camera orientation at shutter time. The viewfinder follows the system rotation lock. The captured composition is preserved when developing and exporting.

Contact support

Open Camera settings → Guide & Privacy → Feedback, or use the English feedback form. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, what you tried and what happened. English feedback opens Google Forms. Chinese feedback opens Tencent Survey.
